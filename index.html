AI in My Work — a Real Automation Problem

Not a demo — production automation that estimators use every day. Here's the specific problem, how I approached it, and what changed.

AI Builder · Power Automate · Copilot

Automating Bill of Quantities (BOQ) Classification

The Problem

Estimators were manually reading hundreds of BOQ line items per project from unstructured Excel exports, categorizing each one by hand, then re-keying them into supplier packages before pricing could even start. It was slow, repetitive, and easy to get wrong — and every downstream step waited on it.

The Approach

I trained an AI Builder model on BOQ item descriptions to classify each line into the correct category automatically, then wired it into a Power Automate flow that packages classified items and routes them into a Model-Driven App for estimator review. Microsoft Copilot's custom prompts summarize supplier responses so estimators can compare quotations faster.

The Result

Manual classification effort dropped by up to 80%, freeing estimators to focus on pricing and negotiation instead of data entry — the same pattern I'd apply to any high-volume manual-classification problem: AI Builder for recognition, Power Automate for orchestration, and a human review step so judgment stays with the estimator, not the model.

Estimators still review every AI-suggested category before it's finalized — the goal was removing repetitive work, not removing judgment.

My Role: Planned and managed the migration approach to make sure large volumes of legacy data could move safely without disrupting day-to-day operations.

The Challenge

Large volumes of legacy data needed to move into Dynamics 365 while the business continued operating — with inconsistent formatting, duplicate records, and no room for downtime during the cutover.

What I Changed

I introduced a more structured migration approach with clear field mapping, data validation, de-duplication rules, and staged testing before the final cutover, instead of a single high-risk move.

How I Worked

I worked through the source data structure, reviewed quality issues with stakeholders, built SSIS (KingswaySoft) packages to map and transform records into Dataverse's schema, and ran validation passes on staging before touching production data.

Business Outcome

More than 500K records were migrated successfully with zero downtime, and the target system received clean, de-duplicated, and validated data from day one.

Before

Legacy & inconsistent

  • Data spread across legacy systems and formats
  • Duplicate and inconsistent records
  • No structured mapping to the new schema
  • High risk of downtime during a single big-bang move
After

Clean & validated

  • Field-level mapping into Dataverse's schema
  • De-duplication and validation rules applied pre-migration
  • Staged testing before production cutover
  • Zero downtime during the final move
