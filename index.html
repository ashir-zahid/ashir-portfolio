Construction / Estimation

AI-Based BOQ Estimation & Tender Automation

My Role: I worked closely with estimators to understand their end-to-end workflow, identified where time was being lost, and helped design and deliver a system that automated the full estimation and tender process while keeping control in the hands of the users.

The Challenge

Estimators were handling the full BOQ process manually through Excel and email. They had to categorize items, send package requests, track supplier replies, compare quotations, and prepare tender settlement by hand.

What I Changed

I helped redesign the process so it could be managed in one system instead of scattered across spreadsheets and inboxes, while still allowing estimators to review and control decisions.

How I Worked

I reviewed the current workflow with estimators, identified bottlenecks, and shaped a more practical process where categorization, supplier communication, quotation handling, and tender preparation were all connected.

Business Outcome

Manual effort dropped by 80%, accuracy improved, and estimators gained full visibility from BOQ upload to final tender submission.

Before

Manual & scattered

  • Excel used for item categorization
  • Email used to send packages to suppliers
  • Supplier quotations tracked manually
  • Alternatives and quantity changes reviewed by hand
  • Tender settlement prepared manually in Excel
After

Centralized & automated

  • BOQ uploaded directly into the system
  • Items categorized automatically using AI
  • Packages sent to suppliers from the platform
  • Suppliers submit quotes through a portal
  • Tender settlement generated automatically

Sales / Compliance / Operations

The business needed more than a basic CRM. Sales processes involved approvals, pricing rules, customer validation, contract generation, and coordination with external platforms. A lot of this work depended on manual checks, repeated data entry, and disconnected systems, which slowed teams down and increased the risk of errors.

What I Changed

I helped shape Dynamics 365 into a central system for managing opportunities, pricing, contracts, validations, and customer data. The focus was not only on sales visibility, but also on reducing manual tasks, improving process control, and connecting CRM with the other systems the business relied on.

How I Worked

I gathered requirements directly from business users, mapped where the process was breaking down, and proposed practical improvements. I introduced automation for approvals, notifications, document generation, and daily data handling, while also supporting integrations for company risk checks, VAT validation, marketing data, and downstream systems. I also worked on pricing setup, product bundles, validations, and record management to make sure the system matched real business needs.

Business Outcome

The result was a more reliable and connected sales platform with better control over customer data, pricing, contracts, and compliance-related checks. It reduced manual work, improved process consistency, and helped shorten the sales cycle by 25% while giving teams better visibility and confidence in the data they were working with.

Customer Service / Case Management

Digital Case Management & Customer Portal

My Role: I worked with business teams to understand how service requests such as building permits and licenses were handled, and helped design and deliver a structured system that automated case handling, task tracking, and team coordination.

The Challenge

Service requests like permits and licenses were handled manually, with limited tracking and unclear ownership. Cases often moved between teams without visibility, deadlines were hard to track, and follow-ups depended on manual effort.

What I Changed

I introduced a structured case management process with clear stages, automatic routing, task tracking, and built-in timelines, making the workflow more predictable and easier to manage.

How I Worked

I mapped the full lifecycle of a case, set up automatic assignment based on case type, introduced stage-based task creation, and implemented SLA tracking with reminders so teams always knew what needed to be done and by when.

Business Outcome

Reduced response time by 30–40%, improved SLA compliance, and ensured clear ownership at every stage. Teams handled cases faster, with fewer delays, and customers gained full visibility into request status.

Before

Manual and unclear process

  • Requests handled manually with limited tracking
  • No clear ownership between teams
  • Deadlines and SLAs difficult to monitor
  • Follow-ups depended on manual reminders
  • No visibility for customers
After

Structured and automated workflow

  • Automatic case routing based on request type
  • Clear ownership at every stage
  • SLA tracking with real-time alerts
  • Automated reminders and task tracking
  • Customer portal for real-time status tracking
